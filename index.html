MasjidHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

At MasjidHub we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we process, why, on what legal basis, and what rights you have, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).

In short: we do not sell your data, we show no advertising, and we use no trackers or analytics. We process only the minimum needed for the app to work and to review the mosques the community contributes.

1. Data controller

  • Owner: Neoprocess
  • Contact (privacy): 1neoprocess@gmail.com

For anything related to your data, write to that address.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties for commercial purposes.
  • We show no advertising.
  • We use no analytics, tracking cookies or third-party trackers.
  • We do not register notification tokens: prayer reminders are local, on your device.
  • Your favourite mosques are stored only on your phone, not on our servers.

4. Who we share data with (processors)

To provide the service we rely on providers acting as data processors, with the safeguards of Art. 28 GDPR:

  • Supabase — database hosting and storage of photos/videos. Servers in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) — Google sign-in.
  • Expo — infrastructure to distribute and update the application.

Some providers (e.g. Google, Expo) may involve international transfers outside the EEA; where that happens, they rely on the appropriate safeguards provided for by the GDPR (Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent frameworks).

We may also disclose data to authorities where there is a legal obligation to do so.

5. Public content

When a mosque you submit is approved by our moderation, its information (including photo and video) becomes publicly visible in the app. Your name and email are NOT shown publicly: they are used only internally for review and to contact you if necessary.

6. Retention period

  • Account data: for as long as your account is active. If you delete it (or ask us to), it is erased; deleting the user cascades to your profile.
  • Approved contributions: may be retained as part of the community directory. At your request, we can anonymise the data linking you to them (author's name and email), keeping the mosque listing for the community.
  • Reports: retained for as long as they are useful for moderation.

7. Your rights

You may exercise at any time your rights of access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), objection, restriction of processing and portability, as well as withdraw your consent. To do so, write to 1neoprocess@gmail.com; we may ask you to prove your identity.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es).

8. Children

MasjidHub is not directed at children under 14. By using the app and contributing content, you declare that you are at least that age (or have your guardians' consent under applicable law).

9. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect your data: encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), row-level security policies (RLS) limiting each user's access to what belongs to them, restricted file uploads, and review of content before publication. No system is 100% infallible, but we work to minimise risks.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the app or in the law. We will publish the current version with its update date and, if the change is significant, we will notify you by reasonable means.

11. Contact

For any question about this Policy or about the processing of your data: 1neoprocess@gmail.com.
